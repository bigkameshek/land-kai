Describe a picture and it appears right in the panel, ready to drag into a doc, a deck, or a chat — no separate image tool, no new tab to lose your place in.

How it works

Three steps. No new window.

01

Click the icon

The panel opens beside your tab, not on top of it — your page stays exactly where it was.

02

Ask anything

About the page in front of you, or about something else entirely. Attach a file if you need to.

03

Keep browsing

The answer stays in view while the rest of the page keeps scrolling underneath it.
